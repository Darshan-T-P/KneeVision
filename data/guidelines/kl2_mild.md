# KL 2 (Mild) — Core Conservative Treatment

KL grade 2 shows definite osteophytes with possible joint space narrowing on
radiographs. This is typically the stage at which patients first present
with consistent symptoms, and where OARSI and AAOS guidelines recommend
starting the "core" conservative treatment package rather than escalating
to injections or surgery.

## Recommendations (core treatments)

- **Structured exercise** is a core, strongly recommended treatment:
  - Strengthening / neuromuscular / cardiovascular ("Type 1") exercise
    programs.
  - Mind-body exercise ("Type 2") such as Tai Chi or yoga, which have
    evidence for pain and function benefit.
  - Aquatic exercise, particularly useful for patients with higher pain
    levels or comorbidities that limit land-based exercise.
  - Supervised programs (e.g. with a physical therapist) tend to show
    better outcomes than unsupervised self-directed exercise.
- **Weight management** if the patient is overweight or obese: even a
  modest weight loss of ~5% of body weight is associated with meaningful
  improvement in pain and function.
- **Self-management education**: arthritis self-management programs that
  build knowledge, self-efficacy, and healthy activity/sleep/stress habits
  are a strongly recommended core treatment, not an optional add-on.
- **Topical NSAIDs** are recommended for localized pain relief with a
  favorable safety profile compared to oral NSAIDs.
- Activity modification (pacing, avoiding high-impact/pivoting activities
  that provoke pain) can help manage symptoms day to day.

## Not yet indicated at this stage

- Bracing/assistive devices are not routinely necessary at KL2 unless there
  is a specific instability or malalignment concern.
- Surgical referral is not indicated at this stage — conservative treatment
  should be tried first and given adequate time (typically several months)
  to show effect.
