# Sources

The guideline documents in this directory are original summaries written for
KneeVision++, synthesizing publicly available clinical practice guidelines.
They are **not verbatim reproductions** of any single guideline, and they are
**not a substitute for clinical judgment** — see the disclaimer below.

Primary sources synthesized:

- Bannuru RR, et al. **OARSI guidelines for the non-surgical management of
  knee, hip, and polyarticular osteoarthritis.** Osteoarthritis and Cartilage,
  2019. https://oarsi.org/education/oarsi-guidelines
- American Academy of Orthopaedic Surgeons (AAOS). **Management of
  Osteoarthritis of the Knee (Non-Arthroplasty), Evidence-Based Clinical
  Practice Guideline, 3rd Edition.** 2021.
  https://www.aaos.org/globalassets/quality-and-practice-resources/osteoarthritis-of-the-knee/oak3cpg.pdf
- Kolasinski SL, et al. **2019 American College of Rheumatology / Arthritis
  Foundation Guideline for the Management of Osteoarthritis of the Hand,
  Hip, and Knee.** Arthritis Care & Research, 2020.
- CDC. **Talking to Patients About Self-Management.**
  https://www.cdc.gov/arthritis/hcp/self-management/index.html
- Arthritis Foundation. **Guidelines for Osteoarthritis Treatments.**
  https://www.arthritis.org/diseases/more-about/guidelines-for-osteoarthritis-treatments

## Disclaimer

This is a research/demo project. The rehabilitation guidance produced by
`kneevision.rag` is an educational summary generated from the documents in
this folder — it is **not medical advice**, has not been reviewed by a
clinician, and must not be used to make real treatment decisions. Always
consult a qualified healthcare professional for actual diagnosis and care.
