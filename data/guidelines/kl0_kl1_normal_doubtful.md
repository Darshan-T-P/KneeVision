# KL 0-1 (Normal / Doubtful) — Prevention and Early Management

At KL grade 0, radiographs show no features of osteoarthritis. At KL grade 1,
changes are "doubtful" — possible small osteophytes only, with no definite
joint space narrowing. Symptoms, if present, are typically mild, and WOMAC
pain/stiffness/function scores are usually lower than in higher grades.

## Recommendations

- **Stay active.** Regular low-impact aerobic activity (walking, cycling,
  swimming) supports joint health and is protective against future symptom
  progression. There is no need for structured physical therapy at this
  stage unless symptoms are present.
- **Maintain a healthy body weight.** Excess body weight increases mechanical
  load on the knee joint and is a modifiable risk factor for OA progression;
  weight management is worth starting proactively, not only after symptoms
  develop.
- **Muscle strengthening**, particularly of the quadriceps and hip
  abductors, improves knee joint stability and may reduce the risk of
  symptomatic progression.
- **Avoid unnecessary imaging or treatment escalation.** A KL 0-1 finding
  with minimal or no symptoms does not warrant bracing, injections, or
  surgical referral.
- **Education** on joint-friendly activity and early warning signs (new
  pain, swelling, stiffness lasting >30 minutes) supports early
  self-management if the condition progresses.
